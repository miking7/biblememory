Bible Copyrights & Acknowledgments

Honoring the translators and publishers who serve the Church

We're deeply grateful for the careful work of Bible translators and publishers. Memorizing Scripture for personal study is something they freely encourage—and below we gladly acknowledge the translations that make this app possible.

Verses You Add Yourself

Any verse you add to Bible Memory is entered by you, in the translation of your choice, for your own personal study and memorization. Bible publishers freely permit this—hiding God's Word in your heart is always encouraged and never restricted. Translation names such as NIV, ESV, and NLT are trademarks of their respective publishers.
